Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

1. Who we are

Edition 11 is a travel and lifestyle marketplace curated by the people you know: members offer homes, cars, boats, tickets and other things for sale or rent, recommend places, and see each other's posts through their friendships.

Edition 11 is run by William Astor ("we", "us"), in the United Kingdom. We decide how your information is used, so we are its controller. You can reach us at ww.astor@gmail.com about anything in this policy.

2. The short version

  • We never sell your information, and there is no advertising beyond Featured items we choose and label as Featured.
  • Your phone number is never shown to anyone.
  • Your list of friends is never shown to anyone. Others see only how many friends they share with you, and the line saying how they know you names only their own friends.
  • Your world, your influence on each friend and who has you in their world are private and never shown.
  • What you save is private and never shown or counted to anyone.
  • Homes are only ever shown at an approximate area.
  • You can delete your account, and everything you posted, at any time from Settings.

3. What we hold, and why

Your account

  • Your mobile number, to sign you in with a code sent by text message, and to keep each account to 1 person. It is never shown to other members. You can change it in Settings, with a code sent to the new number.
  • Your profile: your name, photograph, home town and a short line about you. Your photograph is kept in private storage: it is shrunk and re-saved before it leaves your phone, which removes its location and camera details, and it is shown only through links that expire after an hour.
  • Your settings: who can see your full profile, who can message you, whether you allow followers (turning it off pauses everyone following you until you turn it on again), whether people may find you from their contacts, the size of your world and your notification choices. Your choices of appearance stay on your phone.
  • Your invite code, in the link you can send to friends. Whoever opens it sees your name and photograph.
  • What you write in Report a problem, in Help, with your name, so we can look into it.

Your connections

  • Friendships and friend requests, which need both of you to confirm, and introductions between friends.
  • Who you follow, if they allow followers. Only you can see whom you follow, and nobody can see who follows them.
  • Your influence on each friend (Less, Normal or More), seen only by you.
  • Your world: the people whose posts you see, worked out from your friendships up to 3 steps away, your influence and the size you chose. It is worked out inside our database, and no member can read anyone's world.
  • Blocks, mutes, hidden posts and reports. Nobody is told when you block, mute or report them.

What you post and do

  • Your posts: listings, recommended places, things you share (with any link you add), questions, notices and Looking for, with their photographs, videos and words, and the audience you chose for each. A post is shown only to its audience: Friends only; Your world, the people whose world includes you, which can reach friends of your friends and their friends, as far as the size each of them chose; or Everyone. Recommended places are always shared with Everyone. Before they leave your phone, photographs are re-saved and videos have the place they were filmed blanked out, so neither carries its location, and they are kept in private storage, shown only through links that expire.
  • Places: when you recommend a place or say where a listing is, we keep Google's identifier for the place, and its map position for at most 29 days, as Google's terms allow. Homes are only ever shown at an approximate area.
  • Likes, Recommends (with any note), saves, comments and answers. Likes and Recommends are counted on posts, and the member whose post it is sees who gave them; saves are never shown or counted to anyone. Anyone who can see a post can comment on it.
  • Messages and enquiries, seen only by the members in the conversation.

How you use Edition 11

  • Records of what you do in the app: views (half a post on screen for a second), opens, saves, enquiries, comments, answers, Likes and Recommends, with why the post was in front of you (for example, a friend's post) and where (for example, Home); and other actions, such as changing a setting or following, muting or blocking someone, with that member's identifier. Searches are recorded without what you typed. We use these records to find out whether a marketplace is more useful when items come through the people you know, to improve Edition 11, and to keep it safe. We never use them to build an advertising profile, and the order of your feeds never uses how long you look at anything, how you scroll or what you have viewed before.
  • Your phone's notification address, if you allow notifications, with your choices of which to receive. With "Likes, Recommends and your world" on, once a day at most we send you a summary of the Likes and Recommends on your posts that day, naming who gave them, and on Mondays how much your world grew.
  • Your world's size each night: how many friends, friends of friends and their friends it holds, kept for 2 weeks, so we can tell you each Monday how much it grew.
  • The app's version and update, so we can tell which version a phone has.

Your contacts, only if you ask

  • Find your friends looks through your contacts only if you ask, and with your iPhone's permission. On your phone, each mobile number in your contacts is scrambled with a key we issue for that one search, and only the scrambled numbers are sent. We compare them with the numbers of members who allow it, scrambled the same way, and tell your phone which of your contacts are already on Edition 11. We never store your contacts or the scrambled numbers. We keep only that you looked, for 2 days, so it happens at most once a day, and your phone remembers who it found for 24 hours.
  • Anyone who has your number in their contacts can find you this way, unless you turn off "Let friends find me from their contacts" in Settings. Your number is never shown to them.

What we do not collect

  • Your location. The Map's Find me uses your location only while you use it, on your phone. It is never saved or sent to us. The map itself is Google's, shown under Google's own privacy policy.

4. Our lawful bases

  • To provide Edition 11 to you (our contract with you): your account, profile, connections, posts, messages and notifications.
  • Our legitimate interests, balanced against yours: keeping Edition 11 safe (reports, blocks and stopping misuse), the records of what members do with posts, and improving the service. You can object (section 9).
  • Your consent, given through your iPhone's own questions: notifications, your photographs and camera, and your location. You can withdraw it at any time in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Legal obligations, where the law requires us to keep or share information.

5. Who sees what

  • Other members see what you choose to share, as each post's audience and your settings allow. Every member can see your photograph, name, home town and short line, and find you by name; who can see your full profile, with your posts, is your choice in Settings. Your phone number, your friend list, your world, your influence, your saves and what you have blocked or muted are never shown to them.
  • Our service providers, who handle information only on our instructions:
    • Supabase (our database, file storage, sign-in and server functions), in London.
    • Twilio, which sends sign-in codes by text message, and so receives your mobile number.
    • Expo (650 Industries), which delivers app updates and passes notifications to Apple, including their words, such as who sent you a message and how it begins.
    • Apple, which delivers notifications to iPhones and distributes the app; while Edition 11 is tested through TestFlight, Apple passes on any feedback and crash reports you choose to send.
    • Google, whose maps appear on your phone and which answers place searches: the words you type in a place box are sent to Google through our server, without your name, to suggest places, and are never kept by us.
    • Sentry (Functional Software), which receives a report when the app goes wrong: what failed and where in the app's code, with your iPhone's model and version of iOS. Never your name, number or anything you wrote. Its reports are kept in the European Union.
  • Us, when you report a post, a comment, a member or a problem: we are told at once, so we can act on it.
  • The law: we may share information when the law requires it, or to protect our members or others from harm.

We never sell your information.

6. Outside the UK

Our database is in London, and crash reports are kept in the European Union, which the UK recognises as protecting information properly. Twilio, Expo, Apple and Google may handle some information in the United States and elsewhere. Where they do, the transfer is protected by the UK–US data bridge, for providers certified under it, or by the safeguards UK law requires, such as the International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

7. How long we keep it

  • While your account exists, we keep your account, profile, connections, posts and messages so the service works.
  • When you delete your account (Settings, Delete account), we delete your profile, photographs, videos, posts, comments, answers, messages, Likes, Recommends, saves, friendships, follows, introductions, settings and records, at once. Your conversations and introductions are deleted for the other people in them too, as are the comments, Likes and saves others left on your posts. Reports and problems you sent are kept so we can act on them, without your name.
  • Other times: records of what you do in the app, 12 months; a place's map position, at most 29 days; records of notifications sent, 30 days; your world's nightly size, 2 weeks; counts of place searches, 7 days.
  • Messages stay until you or the other person deletes their account. Friend requests stay until either account is deleted. Listings that have ended and questions that have closed stay in your account, where only you see them, until you remove them or delete your account.
  • A comment you remove disappears for everyone at once, and its words are deleted 30 days later, unless it has been reported and we are still looking into it.
  • A post you remove leaves every feed at once. About 30 days later its words, photographs and videos, and the comments, Likes, Recommends and saves on it, are deleted, unless it has been reported and we are still looking into it; any conversation about it, and its messages, stay with the 2 people in it. Until then it is kept, hidden from everyone, in case it was removed by mistake.
  • Our providers keep their own technical logs, such as records of sign-in codes sent, for their standard periods.

8. Keeping it safe

Every request is checked by our database itself, so nobody can see a post outside its audience, whatever app they use. Photographs and videos are in private storage and shown only through links that expire. Nobody at Edition 11 reads your messages, except when a member reports a conversation or the law requires it.

9. Your rights

You can:

  • see what we hold about you, and take a copy of it: Settings, Download your data, makes the copy at once, as a file you can keep or send to yourself, with links to your photographs and videos that work for 7 days. You can also ask us at ww.astor@gmail.com;
  • correct it, in Edit profile, by changing your mobile number in Settings, or by asking us;
  • delete it, with Delete account in Settings;
  • object to our use of it based on legitimate interests, or ask us to restrict it;
  • take that copy to another service;
  • withdraw consent for notifications, photographs or location in your iPhone's Settings.

We reply within a month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113), though we would like the chance to put things right first.

10. Age

Edition 11 is for people aged 18 and over.

11. Changes

When we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the app before an important change takes effect.
